id: KB-012
title: Pending or duplicate charges after a failed upgrade
---
When an upgrade fails after the card is authorized, your bank may show a pending charge for each
attempt. Pending authorizations that are never captured are released automatically by the bank,
usually within 3-5 business days.

If a charge was captured (not just pending), or if you need the holds released faster, the billing
team can void the authorizations and refund any captured amount. Only the billing team can issue
refunds or void charges; support agents cannot do this from the help desk.

The billing team can also activate the plan manually once one payment is confirmed. Please contact
us before opening a dispute with your bank. A chargeback freezes the account while the bank
investigates, which delays both the refund and plan access.
