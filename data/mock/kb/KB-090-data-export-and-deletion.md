id: KB-090
title: Exporting your data and deleting your account
---
Workspace admins can export all data (contacts, deals, orders and reports) as CSV files from
Settings > Data > Export. The export is emailed to the admin when it is ready.

Requests to delete an account and its personal data (for example under GDPR) are handled by the
privacy team and completed within 30 days. They must come from the account owner's email address,
and the privacy team confirms by email before anything is deleted.
