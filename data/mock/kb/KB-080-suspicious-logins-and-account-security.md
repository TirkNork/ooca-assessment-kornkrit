---
id: KB-080
title: Suspicious logins and compromised accounts
---
If you see a login you do not recognize, change your password immediately, sign out all other
sessions from Settings > Security, and turn on two-factor authentication.

If data may have been accessed or exported by someone else, contact support right away. The
security team reviews the audit log, can lock the account and revoke API tokens, and will tell
you what was accessed. Our staff will never ask for your password or 2FA codes.
