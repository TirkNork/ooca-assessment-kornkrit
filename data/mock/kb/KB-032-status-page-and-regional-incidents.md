title: Status page and regional incidents
---
status.company.com shows the overall health of each region. During the first minutes of a regional
incident, the status page can still show "All systems operational" until the on-call team confirms
the incident and publishes an update.

If you cannot use the product but the status page looks normal, contact support and mention your
region. Support can check the internal monitoring for your region directly.
