id: KB-050
title: Enterprise SLA and priority support
---
Enterprise plans include a 99.9% uptime SLA and priority support. P1 (critical) issues, such as the
service being unavailable for the whole workspace, get a first response within 30 minutes, 24/7.
P1 issues are handed to the engineering on-call team, and a named contact keeps the customer
updated until the issue is resolved.
