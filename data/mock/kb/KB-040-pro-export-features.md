id: KB-040
title: Analytics Pro export features
---
In Analytics, the Pro plan unlocks PDF and PPTX export of dashboards and reports without
watermarks, high-resolution image export, and custom slide sizes. Free accounts can export PNG
files with a watermark.

To export, open the dashboard or report and choose Share > Export, then PDF or PPTX.

The export features become available as soon as the Pro plan is active on the account.
Enterprise plans include every Pro feature.
