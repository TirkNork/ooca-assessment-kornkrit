title: Upgrading from Free to Pro
---
You can upgrade from Settings > Billing > Upgrade. Pro costs $29.99 per month and is activated
immediately after the payment is confirmed.

If the upgrade page shows an error, do not retry more than once. Each attempt places a separate
authorization hold on your card. Contact support instead so the billing team can check the
payment status and activate your plan manually.
